# FLUGIA

**What it is:** a platform of AI co-workers for businesses. Live today: an AI Marketing Manager (David), an AI Customer Support Manager (Emily) and an AI Sales Manager (John). Marked "coming soon": HR (Sarah), Admin/Finance (Caroline) and Assistant (Sofia). They connect to the company's existing tools (CRM, ERP, Google Workspace, Microsoft 365, communication tools), share a "smart memory" knowledge base, and run automations the customer picks. Custom modules can be built by FLUGIA or certified partners.

**Who it is for (stated on the site):** startups, SMEs and large companies. Assumed core market: SMEs with 2 to 50 staff in Belgium, France and the Netherlands (the site is in English, French and Dutch). The pricing code also contains a Morocco price list in MAD.
