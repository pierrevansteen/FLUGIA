# FLUGIA: competitors, market and the gap

Research date: 22 October 2026. Public sources only, linked inline. G2, Capterra, the OpenAI and Google pricing pages and the app stores could not be reached, so some prices come from third-party pages; these are marked (3P). Review quotes are verbatim research, not testimonials. Prices are as published, with no currency conversion.

---

## 1. The verdict, first

**FLUGIA is a credible idea with a weak shop window, in a category that is getting crowded from above and from below.**

- **From above:** in 2026 Anthropic, OpenAI and Google all shipped agents that write to the CRM, run on a schedule and come with ready-made SMB workflows, for about 20–25 USD a seat. [Claude for Small Business](https://www.anthropic.com/news/claude-for-small-business) (May 2026) is almost FLUGIA's exact pitch: 15 ready workflows, HubSpot and QuickBooks connectors, and "you approve before anything sends, posts, or pays", included in paid plans.
- **From below:** [Sintra](https://sintra.ai/pricing) sells 12 "AI helpers" from 15.60 USD/month and [Marblism](https://www.marblism.com/pricing) sells 7 AI employees with unlimited users from 24 USD/month.
- **Beside it, in its own language:** [Limova](https://www.limova.ai/) is French and already has 1,275 Trustpilot reviews at 4.9, 8 agents including a phone agent, TV and metro ads, and an OGC Nice sponsorship.

"AI co-workers for marketing, sales and support" is no longer a differentiator on its own. The gap that is still open is **trust and accountability for European SMEs**: EU hosting you can prove, approval before anything reaches a customer, pricing a buyer can predict, cancellation that just works, and someone who sets it up and answers for the results. Nobody in the category does all five well. The evidence for each point is below.

## 3. FLUGIA, critically

### What is good
1. **The positioning is right.** One subscription covers several departments, with ready-made roles rather than a builder. Eurostat shows marketing and sales as the most common AI use in EU firms (34.7% of AI users) and that 57.9% buy ready-made software rather than build it ([Eurostat 2026](https://ec.europa.eu/eurostat/documents/7870049/23260410/KS-01-26-009-EN-N.pdf/37d063cb-28cf-3b4e-91f3-c3784c970842?t=1774528533658&version=1.1)).
2. **The hybrid model of human onboarding plus a dedicated AI consultant is the strongest asset, and it is under-sold.** "Lack of expertise" is the number one barrier, cited by 70.9% of small EU firms that considered AI and didn't adopt it (same Eurostat source). Limova's 5-star reviews mostly praise its humans, not its AI.
3. **The trilingual FR/NL/EN site and Benelux base.** Belgium is 4th in the EU on AI adoption (34.5%) but has the 2nd-largest gap between large and small firms (76.4% vs 28.8%). That gap is FLUGIA's market.
4. **A free plan, 3 users included, and a partner channel** are sound commercial building blocks.
5. **The FAQ already promises** role-based access, permissions the customer controls, a DPA on request and GDPR practices. The substance exists; it just isn't visible.

### What is not good
1. **Credits are undefined.** The pricing page sells "100 crédits FLUGIA / mois" and "5% off unlimited AI usage" without saying what a credit buys. 70 of the 100 panel buyers said this blocked them. It is also the number one complaint about [Sintra](https://www.trustpilot.com/review/sintra.ai) ("burn through them in 3 days"), [Lindy](https://www.trustpilot.com/review/lindy.ai) ("kill my months credits (3000) in one day!") and [Artisan](https://www.trustpilot.com/review/artisan.co). FLUGIA is copying the category's most hated mechanic.
2. **The proof on the page is fake or empty.** The testimonials in the code (David L., Sarah M., Michael K. "CTO, TechVision") look like placeholders, the footer has "Lorem Ipsum" links, and there is no customer logo, case study or number. "10 times more productive, 20 times cheaper" is unsupported. Gartner warns about "agent washing" and estimates only about 130 genuine agentic vendors among thousands ([Gartner, Jun 2025](https://www.gartner.com/en/newsroom/press-releases/2025-06-25-gartner-predicts-over-40-percent-of-agentic-ai-projects-will-be-canceled-by-end-of-2027); the 130 figure is via [w.media](https://w.media/over-40-percent-of-agentic-ai-projects-could-face-the-axe-by-end-of-2027-gartner/)). An unproven claim plus placeholder reviews reads like exactly that.
3. **Only 3 of 6 co-workers are live.** HR, Finance and Assistant are "coming soon". Limova ships 8, Sintra 12, Marblism 7. Showing empty seats invites the comparison.
4. **The integrations are vague.** "CRM, ERP, Google Workspace, Microsoft 365…" appears with no list. Limova shows about 30 logos and Lindy claims 1,500+. Panel buyers who use HubSpot or Zapier wanted proof of native connectors.
5. **There is no visible approval mode.** Lindy, Artisan, Claude and ChatGPT all show "approve before send". FLUGIA's pitch is "runs 24/7", which for cautious buyers sounds like a risk, not a benefit. 25 panel buyers asked for approval.
6. **EU hosting is hedged.** The FAQ says hosting "dépend de la configuration" and "peut être adaptée" to GDPR. For a European player this should be the headline, not a maybe.
7. **There is no named model or vendor stack.** Buyers can't tell whether it is GPT, Claude or Mistral underneath, and "what does this add over ChatGPT?" came up from 33 panel buyers.
8. **The price-to-proof ratio is off.** At 99 € for 3 users, FLUGIA costs more than Claude Team or ChatGPT Business (≈60–75 $ for 3 seats) and more than Limova (79.90 € HT for **5** users), with less visible product.
9. **The yearly discount reads like a trap.** The 40% off requires a yearly contract, the homepage says the offer "will not be automatically renewed upon renewal", and nothing explains how to cancel. Cancellation is the second complaint theme at both [Limova](https://fr.trustpilot.com/review/limova.ai?stars=1&stars=2&stars=3) ("Impossible de résilier l'abonnement") and Sintra ("Impossible to cancel").
10. **The Morocco pricing is set by GeoIP** (10× in MAD). That's fine, but if it is a real market, it is a different buyer and should be a deliberate segment, not a script.

## 4. Head-to-head

### FLUGIA vs Limova (the most direct threat)
- **Similar:** a French-speaking team of named agents (both have a "John" for marketing or sales), human onboarding, SME focus, a monthly subscription.
- **Limova is ahead on:**
  - **Breadth:** 8 live agents including accounting, legal and recruitment.
  - **A real phone agent:** Tom, at 0.20 €/min, 140 languages, WhatsApp.
  - **5 users included.**
  - **Social proof:** 1,275 reviews.
  - **Marketing:** heavy TV and metro spend, and the OGC Nice partnership.
  - **Model and hosting story:** it says it runs on Claude with data hosted in France, though only in the press.
- **Limova is weak on:**
  - Its own reviews: "trop de bugs non résolus", "Looks more like an MVP", cancellation complaints, onboarding appointments not honoured, and no mobile app.
  - Prices are hidden in a script, refunds are "au cas par cas", and annual plans have no pro-rata refund.
  - No approval controls and no certification.
  - Inconsistent public numbers: 5,000, 10,000 or 30,000 customers depending on the source.
- **Where FLUGIA can beat it:**
  - Belgium and the Netherlands (Limova is France-first).
  - Reliability and accountability: fewer agents, but each one proven.
  - Visible approvals, transparent pricing and painless cancellation.

### FLUGIA vs Sintra
- **Similar:** named helper characters, a shared memory (Sintra's "Brain AI" vs FLUGIA's "smart memory"), credits.
- **Sintra is ahead on:** price (15.60 $/mo yearly), 12 helpers, iOS/Android apps, 100+ languages, $17M in funding and about $12M ARR ([Seedtable](https://seedtable.com/companies/sintra/funding-rounds/seed-2025-06)).
- **Sintra is weak on:**
  - Credit burn, refunds and errors ("It takes more time to fix the mistakes than writing the text").
  - Trustpilot withheld its score for incentivised reviews.
  - US entity and US data processing.
  - It is mostly chat and content, not actions inside the customer's tools.
- **Where FLUGIA can beat it:** don't compete on price. Compete on doing real work inside the customer's systems, under EU rules, with accountability.

### FLUGIA vs Claude, ChatGPT and Gemini
- **Similar:** the same underlying capability. FLUGIA almost certainly runs on one of these model families or a peer.
- **They are ahead on:**
  - Model quality and pace of new releases.
  - Price per seat.
  - Native, maintained connectors (HubSpot builds its own ChatGPT and Claude apps).
  - Certifications: SOC 2, ISO 27001 and ISO 42001 for [Anthropic](https://support.anthropic.com/en/articles/10015870-what-certifications-has-anthropic-obtained).
  - Ready SMB workflow libraries: Anthropic went from 15 to a reported 58 ([Forbes via snippet](https://www.forbes.com/sites/boazsobrado/2026/09/15/anthropic-puts-claude-on-small-business-sales-after-900000-installs/)).
  - Always-on agents with their own computer: [Claude Duet](https://www.cnbc.com/2026/10/21/anthropic-preview-of-claude-duet-personal-ai-agent-take-on-muse-dots.html), ChatGPT Dots.
- **They are weak on (where FLUGIA still has room):**
  - **EU:** claude.ai has no EU residency, OpenAI residency is Enterprise-only, Dots on Pro excludes the EEA, and Duet is a US-only waitlist. Gemini is the exception, with EU data regions.
  - **Do-it-yourself setup:** features are off by default, need admin enablement and write scopes, and third-party steps in Google's Workspace Studio are [beta and off by default](https://workspaceupdates.googleblog.com/2026/09/automate-workflows-with-custom-starters-and-steps-third-party-integrations-and-webhooks-in-Workspace-Studio.html). Nobody sets it up for you or answers for the result.
  - **Platform churn:** OpenAI's AgentKit Agent Builder [shuts down on 30 Nov 2026](https://pickaxe.co/post/openai-agent-builder-deprecation), months after launch.
  - **English-first, US-first** SMB programmes.
- **The honest risk:** for an owner who can configure Claude Team or ChatGPT Business, FLUGIA's generic features (draft emails, summarise, update the CRM, daily brief) are now free with something they already pay for. FLUGIA must sell what those vendors don't: **done-for-you, EU-compliant, accountable outcomes**.

### FLUGIA vs the specialists (Intercom Fin, Artisan, HubSpot Breeze)
- Each one beats FLUGIA at its single job: Fin claims 76% resolution across 12,000+ customers ([fin.ai](https://fin.ai/)).
- But they bill per seat **plus** per outcome (0.99 $ for Fin), or behind HubSpot Pro at 1,300 $+/mo. For a 5-person firm that is either unpredictable or out of reach.
- FLUGIA's case is "good enough at three jobs, one predictable bill".

### FLUGIA vs the builders (Lindy, Zapier, Relevance, n8n/Make)
- **Lindy has the best approval features in the category** but scores 2.1 on Trustpilot, over credits and billing.
- **Zapier** scores 1.3, over support and charges.
- **Relevance** has moved to enterprise-only.
- Builders suit tinkerers. Panel buyers who already had Zapier or Make rejected FLUGIA as "a wrapper". Don't chase them; integrate with them.

## 7. Market research: what companies actually want

**Adoption is rising but uneven** ([Eurostat 2026](https://ec.europa.eu/eurostat/documents/7870049/23260410/KS-01-26-009-EN-N.pdf/37d063cb-28cf-3b4e-91f3-c3784c970842?t=1774528533658&version=1.1), 2025 data, firms with 10+ staff):

| | All firms | Small (10–49) | Large |
|---|---|---|---|
| EU | 20.0% | 17.0% | 55.0% |
| Belgium | 34.5% | 28.8% | 76.4% |
| France | 18.2% | 15.0% | 58.0% |
| Netherlands | 33.2% | 28.8% | 67.6% |

- **France, Bpifrance Le Lab:** 31% of TPE/PME (very small and small-to-medium firms) used generative AI at the end of 2024, but only 8% regularly ([Le Lab](https://lelab.bpifrance.fr/31-des-tpe-et-pme-utilisent-l-ia-generative/)). A relay reports 55% using it, 17% regularly, at the end of 2025, often with free tools ([nxlforge](https://nxlforge.com/blog/55-des-tpe-pme-utilisent-l-ia-la-moitie-sur-des-outils-gratuits)); this is unverified at source.
- **Most use is still casual.** In the US, 80% of small firms use AI but only 18% call it core to operations ([QuickBooks, Jul 2026](https://quickbooks.intuit.com/r/small-business-data/small-business-insights/)). That gap between trying AI and depending on it is the opportunity.

**Why SMEs don't adopt** (Eurostat, small firms that considered AI):
1. Lack of expertise: 70.9%
2. Unclear legal consequences: 54.8%
3. Data protection and privacy: 52.5% (up from 48.8%)
4. Data availability or quality: 43.7%
5. Incompatible with existing systems: 41.7%
6. Too expensive: 38.8% (up from 33.3%)
7. Not useful: only 19.2%

Price is sixth. **SMEs aren't saying "AI isn't useful". They're saying "I don't know how, I'm afraid of the legal side, and it doesn't fit my tools."** All three are service problems, and a service can solve them.

**What gets automated first:**
- Customer service is the most consistent first agent use case: 26.5%, top in the [LangChain survey](https://www.langchain.com/state-of-agent-engineering).
- Then written content and marketing: 68% of French SME generative AI use is writing ([Le Lab](https://lelab.bpifrance.fr/31-des-tpe-et-pme-utilisent-l-ia-generative/)).
- Then decision support and information retrieval ([Upwork, Q1 2026](https://www.upwork.com/resources/state-of-ai-in-smbs)).
- SME owners on Reddit call lead generation and scheduling "mostly hype" for now.

**The agent market is big, and so are the failures:**
- About 7.8 bn $ in 2025, rising to about 52.6 bn $ by 2030 ([MarketsandMarkets](https://www.globenewswire.com/news-release/2026/08/20/3348589/0/en/ai-agents-market-surges-to-52-62-billion-at-a-cagr-46-3-by-2030-report-by-marketsandmarkets.html)).
- Gartner expects more than 40% of agentic projects to be cancelled by 2027.
- Gartner also expects 40% of enterprises to demote or decommission autonomous agents by 2027 because of governance gaps ([May 2026](https://www.gartner.com/en/newsroom/press-releases/2026-05-26-gartner-says-applying-uniform-governance-across-ai-agents-will-lead-to-enterprise-ai-agent-failure)).
- Gartner says 50% of firms planning to cut support staff because of AI will drop those plans ([Jun 2025](https://www.gartner.com/en/newsroom/press-releases/2025-06-10-gartner-predicts-50-percent-of-organizations-will-abandon-plans-to-reduce-customer-service-workforce-due-to-ai)).
- So "replace your staff" is the wrong promise. "Support your team, under control" is the right one.

**Regulation is now a selling point:**
- **EU AI Act Art. 50** transparency has applied since **2 August 2026**: people must be told they are talking to an AI. The Digital Omnibus, Regulation (EU) 2026/1744, delayed the high-risk rules but **not** Art. 50 ([Gibson Dunn](https://www.gibsondunn.com/eu-ai-act-omnibus-agreement-postponed-high-risk-deadlines-and-other-key-changes/), [aiactblog.nl](https://www.aiactblog.nl/en/posts/article-50-transparency-deadline-2-august-2026); check the Official Journal text).
- **Art. 4 AI literacy** has applied since February 2025, in a softened form.
- **The GDPR has not changed:** the GDPR part of the Omnibus is not adopted ([Acompli, Sep 2026](https://acompli.ie/news/digital-omnibus-gdpr-cookies-status-september-2026/)).
- A support or sales agent that discloses it is an AI by default and logs what it said is compliance built in. US tools leave that to the customer.

**Subsidies can lower the price** (verify before using in sales):
- **Wallonia:** Start IA (50% of a scoping mission, up to 5k €) and Tremplin IA (50% of a proof of concept, up to 20k €). The 2026 calls closed in July and September; prepare for 2027 ([Digital Wallonia](https://www.digitalwallonia.be/ia/)).
- **Flanders:** the KMO-portefeuille no longer funds digital advice, but still funds **training** at 30% for small firms, up to 7.5k €/yr ([VLAIO](https://www.vlaio.be/nl/subsidies-financiering/subsidiedatabank/maatregelen/kmo-portefeuille-steun-voor-opleiding-en-advies)). It reportedly ends on 1 January 2027.
- **France:** "Osez l'IA" diagnostics and Bpifrance's Prêt Boost IA ([in-business](https://www.in-business.fr/plan-osez-ia-diagnostics-pme-2026/)).
- **Netherlands:** MIT AI and SLIM. The 2026 windows have closed ([RVO](https://www.rvo.nl/subsidies-financiering/mit/rd-samenwerkingsprojecten-ai)).

## 8. The gap: what nobody does well that FLUGIA could

Each point is tied to the evidence above.

1. **A credit or outcome model a buyer can understand in 10 seconds.** Every credit-based competitor is punished for this in reviews. Options:
   - Replace credits with plain units: "100 credits = about 300 support replies or 40 posts or 150 lead follow-ups". Show a live usage meter, warn at 80%, and never cut off mid-month without warning.
   - Or drop credits: a flat price per co-worker with a fair-use limit stated in tasks.
2. **Approve-before-send on every plan, including Free.** Lindy has it but is US-hosted and poorly rated. Zapier's approvals are Enterprise-only, and Limova and Sintra have none. Make "Nothing reaches your customer until you tap Approve" the headline. Let trust grow from approve-everything, to auto-approve routine replies, to fully autonomous, per co-worker.
3. **EU-only processing you can prove.** Name the hosting region and the models, for example Mistral or EU-region Claude via Bedrock or Vertex. Publish the sub-processor list, a signed DPA you can download in FR/NL/EN, and "never used for training". Only Gemini and Mistral match this among the big players, and neither does done-for-you SME agents.
4. **AI Act Art. 50 compliance built in.** Disclose "I'm FLUGIA's assistant for [company]" by default, keep a log of every customer-facing message, and add an AI-literacy module for the customer's staff (Art. 4). Sell it as "AI Act-ready". No competitor in the scan markets this.
5. **"Done for you in 48 hours" as the product, not a bonus.** The top barrier is expertise (70.9%). Offer a fixed-scope launch: the team connects the tools, loads the knowledge base and switches on 3 automations, with a written result target for day 14. Make it invoiceable as **training or consulting** so it fits KMO-portefeuille training funding, Start IA or Tremplin IA, and Osez l'IA.
6. **Measured results, reported weekly.** Report hours saved, replies sent, leads followed up and response time, compared with before. Gartner's governance warning and Reddit's "generic, needs babysitting" complaint both call for proof. Offer a money-back guarantee if the day-30 target is missed.
7. **One-click cancellation and monthly plans by default.** This beats Limova's and Sintra's worst reviews, and the yearly discount can stay as an option.
8. **Go deep on one or two segments instead of six departments.**
   - Pick the segments where Belgian small firms lag and where support and sales repeat daily: for example, trades and home services with WhatsApp and phone follow-up, or accounting and real-estate agencies.
   - Hospitality (12%) and construction (10.8%) have the lowest AI adoption in Eurostat, so they are the least crowded but the hardest to convert.
   - Ship industry packs ("FLUGIA for real-estate agencies") with pre-written automations and local integrations, rather than generic managers.
9. **Benelux-native integrations the US tools ignore.** Examples: Teamleader, Odoo, Exact Online, Yuki or Octopus/Billit, Peppol e-invoicing (mandatory B2B e-invoicing in Belgium since 2026), itsme, and Bol.com for e-commerce sellers. Verify these with customers before building. These are things Claude, ChatGPT, Gemini and Sintra won't do for a 5-person firm in Liège.
10. **Model-agnostic and churn-proof.** Promise "we switch the best model in for you" as a benefit. Customers are shielded from OpenAI's GPTs → AgentKit → Workspace Agents churn and from US-only feature launches.

**If FLUGIA does none of this,** the honest assessment is that there is no gap. It would be a smaller, newer Limova with fewer agents and no reviews, selling at a higher price than ChatGPT and Claude.
