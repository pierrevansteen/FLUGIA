## What would flip a no

- A monthly plan I can cancel anytime at around 39-49 €, with a clear explanation of what a credit buys, plus a reference from a small business near me showing hours actually saved.
- A month-to-month trial where their onboarding team actually sets up one real thing, like answering my customer emails, and I can see the hours it saves, plus a couple of Belgian or Dutch small businesses like mine I can call who've used it for a year.
- If the onboarding team set it up on my own CRM and inbox during a trial and showed me real leads answered or hours saved within two weeks, before I pay.
- Seeing it in the demo connected to my own inbox and tools, chasing up a real lead on its own, with a clear idea of how many tasks 100 credits covers.
- Seeing it actually running in my own CRM and inbox within an hour, with the onboarding team doing the setup, and a concrete example of hours saved per week compared with just using ChatGPT.
- Seeing it working on my own setup in the free demo, answering my real customer emails and posting for me, with a clear idea of how many replies and posts 100 credits covers.
- Seeing it hooked into my actual CRM and inbox in a demo, doing a real week of follow-ups and posts without me copy-pasting, with a clear idea of how far 100 credits goes.
- Trying the free plan on my real support inbox and seeing it save me a few hours a week, with data hosted in the EU and a clear data processing agreement.
- A clear data-processing page with EU hosting, a signed DPA, and a demo on my own CRM showing a co-worker doing something I can't do by copy-pasting into ChatGPT.
- Seeing it handle my own customer support inbox for a couple of weeks on the free plan with almost no fixing from me, plus a clear explanation of what 100 credits actually covers.
- A small business owner like me, in Belgium or France, telling me it's worked for them for a year, plus a setting where nothing goes out to a customer without me approving it first, the same way I sign off invoices on Fridays.
- A demo on my own inbox where every reply waits for my approval before it goes out, plus a signed data processing agreement with EU hosting and a Belgian or Dutch client of my size I can call.

Buyers say they would buy **1.0 times** in the first month on average.
